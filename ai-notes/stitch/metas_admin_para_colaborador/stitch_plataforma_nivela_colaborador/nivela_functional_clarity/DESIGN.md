## Brand & Style

This design system serves an adult workforce engagement and diagnostic skill-leveling platform. The visual tone balances institutional credibility with radical cognitive accessibility: calm, dignified, reassuring, and deliberately free of infantilizing visual tropes.

### Design Principles
- **Respectful & Adult:** Eliminates cartoonish gamification, condescending praise copy, and hyper-saturated juvenile accents. Accomplishments are marked with quiet authority and clarity.
- **Cognitive Ease & Ergonomics:** Generous touch targets (48px minimum vertical height), explicit iconography paired with text labels, and unambiguous hierarchical grouping.
- **Confidence Through Contrast:** Adheres to WCAG 2.1 AAA for core body text and AA for functional accents, ensuring visual comfort across divergent screen qualities and varying visual acuities.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts utilize structural cards with soft, tinted boundaries rather than busy drop shadows, maintaining a predictable flow of information.

## Colors

The palette establishes an atmosphere of psychological safety and professional gravitas. The primary brand anchor is a deep petroleum teal, balanced against an amber-gold accent reserved exclusively for progress, milestone validation, and completion states.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0F5E66`):** The operational core. Applied to key interactive controls, global navigation headers, and foundational brand markers.
- **Secondary (`#E0A21B`):** Warm amber gold. Used with restraint for achievement badges, streak summaries, and visual indicators of competency milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#1E7E56`):** Gentle forest green. Delivers non-punitive, supportive confirmation states and successful completion feedback.
- **Destructive / Alert (`#D9534F`):** Muted terracotta coral. Communicates issues and corrections cleanly without triggering stress responses.

### Neutral & Canvas Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F8F6`):** A tinted off-white that minimizes screen glare during extended learning sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces that lift content cleanly off the canvas.
- **Surface Stroke (`#E6EAE8`):** Low-contrast structural lines and borders separating distinct conceptual blocks.
- **High-Contrast Text (`#1A2E30`):** Deep petroleum-slate delivering an accessible contrast ratio (>10:1 against white) for long-form reading.
- **Secondary Support Text (`#4A5B5C`):** Subdued neutral providing legible context, secondary labels, and metadata (>4.5:1 ratio).

### Diagnostic Layer Architecture
The three-tier leveling paradigm uses contextual color anchors:
- **Camada 1 (Base - Fundamental Competencies):** Slate Cyan (`#236E80`).
- **Camada 2 (Empresa - Organizational Norms):** Petroleum Teal (`#0F5E66`), accompanied by the high-visibility "Obrigatório" pill tag.
- **Camada 3 (Setor - Specialized Department Track):** Deep Emerald Teal (`#167264`).

## Typography

The design system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project clarity, human warmth, and modern functional legibility without visual distraction.

### Legibility Rules
- **Minimum Body Size:** Standard narrative text and interactive response alternatives must not drop below 16px (`body-md`), ensuring seamless reading without pinch-to-zoom on mobile viewports.
- **Line Heights:** Set generously (150%–165% of font-size) to prevent optical fatigue in longer scenario questions and onboarding descriptions.
- **Paragraph Lengths:** Restrict long-form evaluation prompts to an optical maximum width of 68 characters per line.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an intentional container grid model anchored by an 8pt structural rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column fluid grid, 1200px max container width, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) margins.
- **Tablet (600px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) outer canvas margins.
- **Mobile (<600px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) margins. Multi-column action panels stack vertically.

### Vertical Rhythm
Containers and content blocks prioritize open space over compact information density. Modules separate using `space-xl` (40px) to prevent cognitive overload during diagnostic tasks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through quiet tonal separation and soft, ambient diffusion rather than harsh drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Off-white base (`#F7F8F6`). Used for primary backgrounds and full-bleed stage wrappers.
- **Level 1 (Card Surface):** Pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border using `#E6EAE8`. No shadow in resting state.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Focus / Hover):** Retains `#FFFFFF` surface with an ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 94, 102, 0.08)`. The border shifts smoothly from `#E6EAE8` to `#236E80`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient tinted drop: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(26, 46, 48, 0.12)`, supported by a 40% opacity neutral-dark overlay backdrop.

## Shapes

The shape system employs rounded corners (8px to 16px) to reinforce an approachable, welcoming aesthetic while maintaining crisp institutional form.

- **Primary Interactive Elements:** Inputs, selection controls, and standard buttons utilize `rounded` (8px / `0.5rem`).
- **Cards & Functional Containers:** Diagnostic tracks, summary blocks, and modular containers use `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`).
- **Tags & Badges:** Micro-labels, layer identifiers, and category pills adopt complete encapsulation (`9999px` / pill form) to distinguish them immediately from rectangular interactive cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Touch Ergonomics:** All actionable buttons have a minimum vertical touch area of 48px (`h-12`).
- **Primary Variant:** `#0F5E66` background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold weight (`label-lg`), with 24px horizontal padding. Focus ring features a 2px offset in `#236E80`.
- **Secondary Variant:** White background with a 1.5px solid border in `#0F5E66` and matching text.
- **Accomplishment Action:** `#E0A21B` background with `#1A2E30` high-contrast text for critical milestone handoffs and graduation prompts.

### Cards & Diagnostic Containers
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px outline (`#E6EAE8`), 16px border-radius, and generous internal padding (minimum 24px on desktop, 16px on mobile).
- Track cards feature a 4px left-edge accent stroke colored according to the diagnostic tier: Base (`#236E80`), Empresa (`#0F5E66`), or Setor (`#167264`).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Minimum height of 48px with 16px horizontal internal clearance.
- Borders use `#E6EAE8` at rest, transitioning to a 2px `#0F5E66` outline on focus.
- Labels are persistently visible above the field in `#1A2E30` (weight 600). Helper text and error hints remain permanently anchored below the input in 14px size.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Enlarged hit areas: visual control size is 24x24px, with an interactive click boundary of 48x48px.
- Unselected state uses `#E6EAE8` outline against a white core. Selected state fills with `#0F5E66` featuring a sharp white check/dot. Never rely on color change alone—shape changes and outlines must always accompany selection.

### Badges & Layer Chips
- Always paired with an explicit icon and uppercase text label.
- **Camada Empresa Badge:** Encapsulated pill in `#0F5E66` with high-contrast text and a secondary lock or shield icon denoting mandatory company compliance.
- **Achievement Chips:** `#E0A21B` tinted base (15% tint) with `#8A6000` text for readability without visual loudness.
